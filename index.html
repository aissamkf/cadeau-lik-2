✦ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the estimated delivery date?

You choose the delivery date.

Please order at least 3 days in advance.

Where can I pay the advance payment?

Custom orders require an advance payment.

After submitting the order form we will send bank transfer details on WhatsApp.

Can I customize my own gift?

Yes — use the "Build Your Own Box" option to pick your items and send your request on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver across Morocco?

Yes — we deliver to all major cities including Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Fes, Tangier, Agadir, and more.

Can I include a personal message with the gift?

Absolutely. Every order includes a personal card field in the order form where you can write a custom message for the recipient.
